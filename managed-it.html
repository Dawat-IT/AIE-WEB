How it starts

From first call to steady operations

The same four steps apply whether you hire us for a month or for years.

  1. Review

    A short call and a walk through your systems, users and pain points. You leave with a written list of what to fix first.

  2. Stabilize

    We fix the issues that cause the most disruption, set up monitoring and backups, and document what we find.

  3. Operate

    Day-to-day support begins under agreed response targets, with every request tracked to completion.

  4. Improve

    Each month we review recurring problems, find the cause, and fix it for good. You get a short report on what changed.

Questions

Managed IT, answered

Who is Managed IT for?

Small and mid-size businesses in the United States that need dependable IT without building a large internal team. We also support larger teams that need extra operational capacity.

How is Managed IT priced?

As a fixed monthly fee based on your number of users, systems and coverage hours. You receive a written quote after the free IT review.

Will you replace our current IT person or provider?

Only if you want us to. Many clients keep an in-house contact and use us for monitoring, after-hours support, process or projects.

How do you handle access to our systems?

We work through named accounts with the minimum permissions needed, with written approval for administrative access. Access is reviewed regularly and removed when work ends.

How fast do you respond?

Response and resolution targets are set by priority in your agreement, so they match your business and the plan you choose.
