Questions

Enterprise ITSM, answered

Which tools do you work in?

The ones you already use, including ServiceNow and Jira. We improve the process inside your tool rather than asking you to switch.

Is an ISO 27001 review a certification audit?

No. It is a readiness and gap review that compares your current practices with the standard's requirements and gives you a written findings list and action plan. It prepares you for a certification audit by an accredited body.

Can you work alongside our internal team?

Yes. We can advise, run part of the process, or take on specific roles such as incident coordination or problem management for a period.
