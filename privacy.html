Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Last updated October 22, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by AMZM Global LLC, which does business as American IT Experts ("we", "us"). Our address is 3401 Custer Rd, Suite 154, Plano, TX 75023, USA. You can reach us at support@americanitexperts.com or (469) 326-2287.

What we collect

Information you give us. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, work email, company, the service you are interested in, and your message. If you use the website assistant to leave your details, we receive your name, email and the need you describe. If you email or call us, we keep what you tell us.

Technical data. Our hosting provider and the services that deliver this site may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you request. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.

Data kept on your device. The site remembers your light or dark display choice, and keeps your conversation with the assistant while your browser tab is open. This stays in your browser. It is not shared with us, and you can clear it by clearing your site data. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, set up a call, and prepare a quote.
  • To provide, support and improve our services and this website.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

The website assistant

The assistant is automated, not a person. It currently answers from fixed text on this site. If we turn on AI-generated answers, the questions you type are sent to our AI service provider to produce a reply. Please do not type passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into the assistant or the contact form.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to you, such as website hosting, form handling, email, and, if switched on, an AI service. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also share information when the law requires it, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including keeping a record of our business dealings with you. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law. To make a request, write to support@americanitexperts.com. We may need to confirm who you are before we act on it, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No website or email service is completely secure, so please share only what you need to.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Other websites

This site links to other websites, such as Google Maps. They have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
