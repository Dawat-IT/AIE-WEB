Questions

Before you get in touch

Not here? Ask the assistant, or send us a note.

Who do you work with?

Small and mid-size businesses in the United States that need dependable IT or software without building a large internal team. We also support larger organizations that need extra operational capacity or ITSM expertise.

Do you only support AI and CRM tools, or build them too?

Both. We build AI agents, chatbots and automations made for your business, and we support the CRM you already use, including Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365.

How much does it cost?

Managed IT is a fixed monthly fee based on your users, systems and coverage hours. Projects are quoted at a fixed scope. After the free IT review you receive a written quote.

How fast do you respond?

We reply to new enquiries by the next business day. For supported clients, response and resolution targets are set by priority in your agreement.

Will you replace our current IT person or provider?

Only if you want us to. Many clients keep an in-house contact and use us for monitoring, after-hours support, process or projects.

How do you handle access to our systems?

We work through named accounts with the minimum permissions needed, with written approval for administrative access. Access is reviewed regularly and removed when work ends.

Tell us what keeps breaking.

Send a short note about your systems and the problems you want gone. We will tell you where we would start.
